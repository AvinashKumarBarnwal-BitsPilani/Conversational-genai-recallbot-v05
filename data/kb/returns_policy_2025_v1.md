ShopSphere Returns and Refunds Policy — 2025
Document ID: KB-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 | Status: superseded

Purpose and scope
This archived policy records the return rules that applied to ShopSphere orders delivered during calendar year 2025. It is retained for historical questions and audit comparisons. It must not be applied to orders delivered on or after 1 January 2026. When a customer asks for the current rule, use KB-002 instead. The order delivery date, not the date on which a customer first browses the help centre, determines which version applies.

Return eligibility in 2025
Most unused physical products could be returned within 14 calendar days after delivery. The customer had to include the original accessories, manuals and retail packaging where practical. Products showing accidental damage, liquid exposure, missing serial labels or signs of unauthorised repair could be rejected after inspection. A return request created after the 14-day period was normally ineligible unless a statutory right or a documented service exception applied. The 2025 electronics rule was stricter: headphones, speakers, chargers and other electronic accessories had a 7-calendar-day return window from delivery. This historical 7-day electronics window is not the current rule.

Items excluded from ordinary change-of-mind returns
Personalised products, opened hygiene products, downloadable software after activation, gift cards and items marked Final Sale were excluded from ordinary change-of-mind returns. A damaged, defective or incorrectly supplied item could still be reviewed under the applicable consumer-rights process. A support agent could not promise an exception merely because a product was excluded from change-of-mind returns.

Return request and inspection
Customers submitted a request from Order History or contacted support with the order ID and item. ShopSphere issued a return merchandise authorisation (RMA) after eligibility checks. The parcel was inspected after it reached the returns centre. The inspection outcome could be accepted, partially accepted or rejected, with a reason recorded against the RMA. A customer could ask for an explanation of the inspection decision.

Refunds under the archived policy
For an accepted return, the refund was initiated after inspection. The refund destination normally followed the original payment method, subject to payment-network limitations. The old policy did not guarantee that a bank or card issuer would post the credit on the same day. Customers were advised to retain the refund reference until the credit appeared on their statement.

Version handling
This policy is kept so that an assistant can explain why an older order received a different eligibility result. For current orders, do not quote the 2025 electronics window of 7 days as if it were still active. The current policy is KB-002, version 2.0, effective 1 January 2026. If the delivery date is missing, ask for the order ID and look up the recorded delivery date before deciding which version applies.
