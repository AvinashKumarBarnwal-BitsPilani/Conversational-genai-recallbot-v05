ShopSphere Membership Benefits Guide
Document ID: KB-012 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Status: active

Membership tiers
ShopSphere currently offers Basic, Plus and Gold membership tiers. The tier displayed in the customer profile is authoritative; a customer statement alone does not change the account tier. Basic members receive standard order tracking and access to ordinary support channels. Plus members may receive selected shipping promotions and priority support for eligible orders. Gold members may receive free standard shipping on eligible baskets, priority chat routing and an extended service window for selected product categories where the promotion terms explicitly say so.

Benefits are conditional
Membership benefits vary by category, seller, basket amount and postal code. A free-shipping benefit is not guaranteed on bulky goods, remote-area deliveries or marketplace items excluded by the offer. Priority support is a queueing benefit, not a promise of immediate resolution. An assistant should read the benefit eligibility for the actual customer and order before claiming a discount or service level.

Profile facts and preferences
A customer's communication preference, saved delivery window and delivery note are profile data separate from membership tier. A customer can request an update or deletion of these preferences. Do not infer a communication channel from the last message alone; use the current saved preference unless the customer updates it. A forget request must remove the targeted profile fact and any memory references that would reproduce it later.

Promotions
Membership promotions may have start and end dates. A promotion that has expired must not be described as currently available. When multiple offers apply, the checkout service determines the final combination. The assistant may explain published terms but should not calculate an unverified final price.

Privacy and account access
The assistant may use membership information only after the customer is authenticated in the application context. Do not reveal another customer's tier, address or order information. If identity or ownership is uncertain, ask the customer to sign in through the official application and do not bypass the account access check.
