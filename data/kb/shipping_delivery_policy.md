ShopSphere Shipping and Delivery Policy
Document ID: KB-005 | Version: 2.2 | Effective: 2026-03-01 | Status: active

Delivery services
ShopSphere offers Standard, Express and Scheduled delivery in supported postal codes. Standard delivery normally takes 3 to 6 business days after dispatch. Express delivery normally takes 1 to 2 business days after dispatch. Scheduled delivery provides a customer-selected window from the available slots returned by the delivery service. The estimate shown at checkout is a forecast and can change if a carrier reports a disruption. The order detail page remains the source of truth for the current shipment status.

Delivery windows and preferences
A customer may save a preferred delivery window, but a preference does not guarantee that every carrier route can honour it. Available delivery slots must be checked for the specific postal code and shipment. If a window is unavailable, offer the nearest available slot or ask the customer to select another day. The account preference is a default for future orders and can be updated or deleted on request. The assistant must distinguish a stored preference from a confirmed slot for a particular shipment.

Delivery instructions
Customers may add a short delivery instruction, such as calling on arrival or leaving a parcel with a named reception desk, where the building and carrier permit it. Instructions must not ask a carrier to leave a parcel in an unsafe, publicly accessible or weather-exposed location. A customer can ask for a saved delivery note to be changed or forgotten. Once a note is deleted from the profile store, it must not be repeated from conversation memory. The assistant should not infer a new delivery note from an old one.

Delivery exceptions
A carrier scan of “attempted” means a delivery attempt was recorded; it does not prove that the customer was contacted. “Address issue” means the carrier could not validate the address or access instructions. “Held at depot” means the parcel is awaiting a carrier action or customer instruction. For a missed attempt, the customer may use the reschedule options shown in the order service. The assistant must not promise a second attempt without checking the tracking record.

Late or lost shipments
If the estimated delivery date has passed, retrieve the latest carrier scan and compare it with the promised window. A delay of one business day can be monitored. A parcel with no scan movement for 48 hours should be referred to the carrier desk. A parcel with no movement for five calendar days may qualify for a lost-shipment investigation. Do not mark an order lost solely because the customer says it has not arrived.

Fees and restrictions
Shipping fees are calculated at checkout based on service, basket and postal code. Free-shipping eligibility may depend on membership or promotion terms. Remote-area restrictions and product handling requirements can remove Express or Scheduled options. Only quote a fee returned by checkout for the actual order; examples in help content are not customer-specific prices.
