ShopSphere Damaged, Incorrect and Missing Item Procedure
Document ID: KB-010 | Version: 1.2 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Status: active

Report window and evidence
Customers should report visible transit damage, an incorrect item or a missing item as soon as possible, preferably within 48 hours of delivery. This recommended reporting period helps preserve carrier evidence; it is not a statement that statutory rights disappear after 48 hours. Ask for the order ID, affected item and a short description. Photos of the outer packaging, shipping label and affected product can help the investigation. Do not ask for unnecessary personal documents.

Damaged item
For a crushed, wet or visibly broken parcel, record the condition of the outer box and the item. If the product could be unsafe, advise the customer not to use or charge it. Create a damage case only after checking the order and item. The returns team decides whether to arrange a replacement, refund or inspection. The assistant should not promise a particular remedy before the case is reviewed.

Incorrect item
If the item delivered does not match the order line, compare the SKU and description in the order service with the product received. A wrong-item case should preserve both the ordered SKU and the reported received item. The customer should keep the packaging until the carrier or returns team provides instructions. Never tell the customer to send an unrelated item without a valid return authorisation.

Missing item in a multi-item order
A shipment may contain multiple parcels. First check whether the order has more than one shipment and whether the missing line is still in fulfilment. If all parcels are marked delivered, open a missing-item investigation. The assistant should not assume theft or accuse the carrier. The case records packing scan, parcel count, carrier proof and customer statement.

Remedy and communication
The available remedy depends on stock, fault assessment and order status. A replacement can be offered only when inventory and service eligibility are confirmed. A refund is initiated according to KB-003 after the case outcome is accepted. The response should explain what evidence is needed, what the next review step is and when the customer can expect an update. Avoid guarantees that are not present in the case system.

Escalation
A safety-related defect, repeated packing error or high-value missing item should be escalated to the relevant operations queue. If the issue involves potential account compromise or payment fraud, use the security route rather than the ordinary returns queue.
