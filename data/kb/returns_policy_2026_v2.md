ShopSphere Returns and Refunds Policy — 2026
Document ID: KB-002 | Version: 2.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Status: active

Purpose and version rule
This is the current ShopSphere returns policy for orders delivered on or after 1 January 2026. It supersedes KB-001 for those orders. The delivery date determines the applicable policy version. A customer who asks about a current order should receive the rules in this document, not the archived 2025 rules. If the delivery date is unknown, retrieve the order before calculating the eligibility window.

Standard return windows
Most eligible, non-personalised products may be returned within 30 calendar days from the recorded delivery date. Electronics and electronic accessories, including headphones, wireless earbuds, speakers, chargers and smart-home accessories, may be returned within 14 calendar days from delivery. The electronics rule is identified internally as RET-E14. The general rule is identified as RET-30. The final day is included until 23:59 in the delivery destination's local time. A request created within the window remains eligible for inspection even if the physical pickup occurs a few days later, provided the pickup is booked within seven calendar days of authorisation.

Condition and packaging
For a change-of-mind return, the item should be unused, complete and in resaleable condition, with included accessories and manuals. Original retail packaging is preferred but is not required where safe transport packaging is available. A product that is defective, damaged in transit or materially different from its listing is assessed under the fault or seller-error path. Do not classify a reported defect as a simple change-of-mind return without asking a short diagnostic question.

Excluded products and exceptions
Personalised goods, activated digital licences, gift cards, opened hygiene products and products labelled Final Sale are not eligible for ordinary change-of-mind returns. This exclusion does not remove a customer's right to report a fault, transit damage or an incorrect shipment. Such cases need evidence and an inspection decision. Only the designated returns supervisor can approve a policy exception; the assistant must not invent one.

Authorisation and pickup
A return request must identify the order and item, and capture a reason. The assistant checks delivery date, item eligibility and order status before offering a pickup. A return authorisation is not a completed return. The item must be handed to the carrier and inspected. A pickup can be booked for an available date within seven calendar days after authorisation. The pickup confirmation must show the item, pickup address, date and time window. A database write to create the pickup must happen only after the customer explicitly confirms the complete summary.

Refund handling
For an accepted return, the refund is initiated after inspection. The refund method follows the original payment method unless a payment-network restriction requires an approved alternative. The refund initiation time and bank posting time are different events. See KB-003 for method-specific estimates. The assistant must distinguish “refund initiated” from “money visible in the account.”

Customer communication
Explain the applicable window, the condition requirement and the next step. If the required order or item information is missing, ask for it rather than making an eligibility promise. When the system cannot verify a rule, say that support review is required and do not fabricate a deadline.
