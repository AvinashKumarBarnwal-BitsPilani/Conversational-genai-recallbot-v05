ShopSphere Shipping and Delivery Policy — 2025
Document ID: KB-016 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 | Status: superseded

Purpose and applicability
This archived shipping policy applied to orders placed from 1 January through 31 December 2025. It is retained to explain historical delivery estimates and must not be used to quote current service levels for orders placed on or after 1 January 2026. For a current order, use KB-005, version 2.2. Check the order creation date and the service displayed at checkout before deciding which policy applies. A customer's saved delivery preference is not itself a carrier booking.

Historical delivery estimates
Under the 2025 Standard service, delivery normally took 4 to 7 business days after dispatch. Express delivery normally took 2 to 3 business days after dispatch in supported postal codes. These were estimates rather than guaranteed arrival dates. The actual carrier scan history remained the source of truth once a parcel left the fulfilment centre. Scheduled delivery was available only in selected service areas and only when the checkout service returned a slot.

Delivery attempts and exceptions
A carrier attempt could be recorded even when the customer did not receive a call or message. Customers were asked to check the shipment page for a reschedule option. An address validation issue, depot hold or weather disruption could change the estimated arrival date. Support staff had to read the latest carrier scan before explaining a delay and could not manually mark a shipment as delivered.

Delivery instructions
Customers could add a short instruction, such as a safe reception handover, subject to the carrier's building-access rules. The carrier could reject instructions that asked for a parcel to be left in an exposed or publicly accessible place. Customers could request that a saved note be updated or removed from their profile. Removal from the profile did not authorise staff to repeat the note in later conversations.

Fees and service restrictions
Shipping fees were calculated at checkout using the service, basket, product handling requirements and postal code. Remote areas and bulky products could be excluded from Express service. Promotional free shipping was subject to the offer terms and did not automatically apply to every seller or product. Support could explain published rules but had to rely on the checkout result for a customer-specific fee.

Version comparison
The current 2026 schedule is faster for some routes: Standard is normally 3 to 6 business days and Express is normally 1 to 2 business days after dispatch. Do not apply those current estimates to a 2025 order, and do not apply this archived 4-to-7-day Standard estimate to a 2026 order. The version must be selected using the order date recorded by ShopSphere.
