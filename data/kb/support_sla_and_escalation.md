ShopSphere Customer Support Service Levels and Escalation
Document ID: KB-014 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Status: active

Support channels
Customers can contact ShopSphere through in-app chat, email and the help centre. Channel availability may vary by time and account tier. A saved preference indicates how the customer would like routine updates; it does not prevent the customer from choosing a different channel during an active interaction. Sensitive account changes must be completed in the authenticated application rather than by replying to an unverified email.

Routine service targets
The standard target for a routine email response is within two business days. In-app chat is queued according to current staffing and does not guarantee an immediate human response. A return inspection update is normally provided within three business days after receipt at the returns centre. These are service targets, not guarantees that every case will be resolved within that time. The assistant should check the case timestamp before stating that a target has been missed.

Escalation categories
Use RETURNS-OPS for RMA, inspection and pickup exceptions; PAYMENTS for refund or settlement mismatches; CARRIER-DESK for parcel movement and missed delivery; CATALOGUE for inaccurate product listings; and ACCOUNT-SECURITY for suspected account takeover or fraudulent payment requests. A case should include the order ID, relevant event reference, a concise issue summary and evidence already supplied. Do not copy passwords, OTPs or full payment credentials into a case.

Priority handling
Safety concerns, suspected fraud, repeated failed delivery attempts and high-value missing items require expedited triage. Membership priority may affect queue order where the account benefits say so, but it does not override security or safety procedures. The assistant must not mark a case as resolved until the owning team records an outcome.

Updates and ownership
A support case has an owner queue, status, created timestamp and next-update target. If an escalation is created, provide the case reference and explain which team owns the next action. If the system cannot create a case, disclose that it could not be completed and provide the approved contact route. Do not claim that an escalation has been submitted without a returned case ID.
