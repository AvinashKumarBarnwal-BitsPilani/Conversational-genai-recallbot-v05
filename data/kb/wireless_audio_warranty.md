ShopSphere Wireless Audio Product and Warranty Guide
Document ID: KB-009 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Status: active

Product family
The AUR-220 is a true-wireless earbud set containing left and right earbuds, a charging case, a USB-C cable and printed safety information. The catalogue SKU is SKU-AUR-220. The standard retail box includes one pair of earbuds and one case. A replacement ear tip is a service part and is not a separate retail SKU in the order history unless it was purchased independently. The AUR-220 supports Bluetooth audio and touch controls; battery performance depends on volume, codec and operating conditions.

Warranty coverage
AUR-220 carries a 12-month limited manufacturer warranty from the recorded delivery date. Manufacturing defects in charging, battery operation, touch controls or Bluetooth connectivity may be eligible for repair or replacement after troubleshooting and inspection. Accidental drops, liquid ingress, unauthorised opening, cosmetic wear and damage caused by incompatible charging accessories are excluded from ordinary warranty coverage. Warranty eligibility is separate from the ShopSphere return window; a product can be outside the return window but still within the manufacturer's warranty period.

Basic diagnostics
If one earbud does not charge, ask the customer to place both earbuds in the case, connect the case to a known-good USB-C power source for at least 30 minutes, clean visible contacts with a dry lint-free cloth and retry. Do not ask the customer to insert metal objects or liquid into the charging port. If one side still fails, record which side, the observed indicator behaviour and whether the case charges. This information supports the warranty inspection; it does not itself prove the product is defective.

Return or warranty path
Within the current 14-day electronics return window, a customer may request a return under KB-002. A reported fault should be marked as defective and may require inspection. After that window, direct the customer to the manufacturer's warranty path if the delivery date and product conditions support it. The assistant should not promise immediate replacement before the warranty service confirms the claim.

Accessory compatibility
Use the supplied or certified USB-C charging cable and a compliant power source. Avoid high-pressure cleaning, immersion or solvents. If the product becomes unusually hot, stop charging and contact support. Include the serial number only in the authorised support form; do not place it in a public chat transcript or unrelated memory record.
